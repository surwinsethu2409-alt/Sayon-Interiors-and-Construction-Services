FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What details do you need for a cost estimate / BOQ?

Architectural drawings (plan, elevation, section), structural drawings if available, and a brief of the scope (residential / commercial / interiors). PDF or AutoCAD files work best. We can also work from site measurements.

How long does a typical estimate or BBS take?

Most residential BOQs and BBS are delivered within 2–5 working days depending on project size and drawing clarity. Urgent requirements can be prioritised.

Do you only do estimation or also interiors & construction?

We provide both. Estimation, quantity take-off, BBS, structural design and plan approval, plus complete interior design & execution and construction planning / site support.

Which materials do you recommend for modular interiors?

We guide on BWP / HDHMR plywood for wet areas, quality laminates or acrylic finishes, and reliable hardware brands so the work lasts. Final selection is done with you based on budget and look.

Do you work only in Trichy or other cities too?

We are based in Karumandapam, Trichy. Estimation, BOQ and design work can be done pan-India (drawings shared digitally). Site visits and execution support are primarily in and around Trichy / Tamil Nadu.

WhatsApp or call +91 99524 06705, or use the contact form on this site. Share your drawings or project brief and we will respond with next steps and indicative pricing.
